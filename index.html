About The Classes

Building confidence, knowledge and examination readiness.

A free O/L Classes series for Ordinary Level (O/L) students, organized by the Old Boys' Association (OBA) of Hameed Al Husseinie College. The main aim is to strengthen subject knowledge and guide students towards higher results.

Classes Features

Everything is arranged to make the learning experience focused, useful and effective.

Dedicated sessions for important O/L subjects.

Experienced Teachers

Revision sessions and paper discussions to improve examination confidence.
